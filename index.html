Why check your Open Graph preview?

When you share a link on social media, the platform reads your page's Open Graph (og:) tags to build the link card. If those tags are missing, malformed, or point to an oversized image, your link renders as a bare URL — and nobody clicks it. OG Preview Checker shows you exactly what your audience sees before you hit post.
